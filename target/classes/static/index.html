Rules & Regulations

1

Game Objective

Tic-Tac-Toe is a classic two-player turn-based strategy game played on a 3×3 grid. The goal is to be the first player to get three of your marks in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally).

How to Play & Multi-Round PvP

  • Starting Turn: Player X (Cross) always makes the opening move of the game.
  • Alternating Moves: Players take turns clicking on any available empty cell to place their mark.
  • No Overwrites: Once a cell is marked with X or O, it cannot be changed for the rest of the round.
  • Multi-Round PvP: In 2-Player mode, after a player wins, the match automatically pushes to Round 2, 3, etc. by resetting the board while maintaining and adding to the winner's cumulative victory tally!
  • Draw/Tie: If all 9 squares are filled without 3-in-a-row, the round is a Draw and advances with points preserved.
3

AI Opponent Difficulties

🟢 Easy

AI plays casual, randomized moves. Best for warm-ups or casual enjoyment.

🟡 Medium

Tactical AI: Actively seizes instant winning moves and blocks your 2-in-a-row threats, while prioritizing center and corner positions.

🔴 Hard (Minimax)

Master AI powered by the recursive Minimax Algorithm with game-tree search. It anticipates all possible futures — mathematically unbeatable!

4

Smart Hint System 💡

Stuck or planning a tactical move? Click the 💡 Get Hint button at any time during your turn. The Java backend will evaluate the board and highlight the optimal strategic cell with a glowing golden border and detailed rationale.
